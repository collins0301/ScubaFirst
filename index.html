80 ft Questions

Before you sign up.

Do I need scuba experience before Open Water?

No. Open Water is the entry-level certification. You'll still need to meet the agency's prerequisites and medical requirements.

Do you teach PADI or SDI?

Both. Our instructors are cross-certified with PADI and SDI. We'll explain the practical differences and help you choose.

Where do you teach?

We're based in Payson and serve all of Utah County. Classroom, pool and open-water locations depend on the course and schedule.

What gear do I need?

It depends on the course. We'll tell you exactly what you need before class. Most students bring their own mask, fins and snorkel unless we arrange otherwise.

Can kids get certified?

Yes. Junior Open Water starts at age 10, with age-specific depth limits and supervision. Introductory scuba experiences start at age 8.

What if I'm nervous?

That's normal. Small classes give you time to practice each skill at your own pace until it feels comfortable.
