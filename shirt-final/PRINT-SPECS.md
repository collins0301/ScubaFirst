# Jackson Dive Family Shirt: Print Specs

**Shirt color:** navy (the artwork has a transparent background, so the shirt shows through the ring)
**Recommended print method:** DTG (direct-to-garment) or DTF, because the water uses color gradients and light rays.
**Quantity:** 6 shirts (Susan, Amber, Russell, Raleigh, Chase, Carter)

## Files to send

| Placement | Print-ready file | Size | Vector / editable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full back | `BACK-print-300dpi-3600x4200.png` | 12 × 14 in at 300 DPI | `BACK-design-12x14in.pdf`, `back-design-EDITABLE.svg` |
| Left chest | `FRONT-chest-print-300dpi-1200x900.png` | 4 × 3 in at 300 DPI | `FRONT-chest-4x3in.pdf`, `front-chest-EDITABLE.svg` |

- The PNG files are ready to print as-is (transparent background).
- The PDF and SVG files are vector, with live text, if the shop wants to adjust anything.
- The SVG files open in Adobe Illustrator, Inkscape, or Affinity Designer. Each part is on its own named layer: Sky, Water, Light Rays, Surface Waves, Dive Boat, Reef Background, Reef Foreground, Diver, Bubbles, Print Texture, Ring, Ring Text, Generation Text, Memorial Text.
- **Fonts:** the `fonts/` folder includes Montserrat, Cinzel and Libre Baskerville. All are free Google fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Install them before editing the SVG, or the text will substitute a different font.

## Colors

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Cream (all text, ring) | `#EFE6D2` |
| Dark navy (boat, diver, reef silhouettes) | `#0E2238` |
| Dive-flag red | `#C9352B` |
| Water | gradient from `#3A9DB6` (surface) to `#0C3352` (bottom) |

## Placement

- **Back:** centered, top of the art about 3 in below the collar seam.
- **Front:** wearer's left chest, about 4 in wide.

`reference/chatgpt-mockup.jpg` is the concept image this artwork was rebuilt from.
